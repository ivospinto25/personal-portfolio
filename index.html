About

AI-Driven Design

Product Designer with 5+ years of experience, currently working as a UI/UX Designer at UMAIN WORKS, where I have worked on projects for clients in AI, oil and gas, healthcare, motorsport, education, communications and consulting. I recently took on the role of Product Owner for an internal product.

My work focuses on UX strategy and design systems, from research through to implementation with the development team. I use tools such as Claude, Lovable and Figma AI to speed up research, definition and prototyping, but decisions still rely on human analysis and validation.

Right now, I am most interested in AI products where people can see what the agent did, understand why, and decide — always with the option to undo. I believe human validation should be part of the product, not a separate step.

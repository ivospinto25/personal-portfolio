01

Overview

Founded by two doctors who know the sector “from the inside”, Medclic is a strategic platform designed to simplify the bridge between doctors and patients. The focus is to keep healthcare professionals close to users, and to make booking an appointment smooth, intuitive and hassle-free.

What problem are we trying to solve?

In healthcare, the wait for a specialist appointment can stretch for months or even years, leaving patients in a vulnerable position.

Healthcare in Portugal faces a critical obstacle: slowness.

Strategic goals

  • Make it easier to connect healthcare professionals and patients.
  • Provide a centralized digital portal where professionals can sign up and manage and maintain their profile.
  • Let patients find and book appointments by specialty and location.
  • Allow medical appointments to be booked online, directly through the platform.
  • Simplify onboarding for healthcare professionals, letting them apply directly from the landing page.
  • Make sure every user is notified by email about confirmations, cancellations or rescheduling.
02

Research

Research methods

In meetings with the founders, we identified pain points that go beyond technology — they touch on the healthcare culture in Portugal:

“We see patients who waited a year for a specialist appointment in the public system and, when they get to the private sector, they get lost in the booking process. The system has to be so simple that it stops feeling like a barrier and becomes an open door.”
“People forget, or don’t bother to cancel. We need a system of automatic alerts and quick confirmations that frees us from admin work and makes sure the schedule has no empty slots while there are people on a waiting list.”
“Our typical patient can be 5, 30 or 70 years old. If the booking process is confusing or feels ‘too techy’, the senior patient gives up and calls the health center again. It has to inspire the same trust as a family doctor, or offer a way to book for family members.”
“We spend half of the appointment filling in forms or validating insurance details. If the platform can collect that information at booking time, we gain precious minutes for what really matters: the patient.”

Key drivers

Integrated family member management

Let users add family members to their account and book appointments on their behalf.

Fast booking flow

A search flow by specialty, location or symptom.

Doctor schedule management

An intuitive dashboard where healthcare professionals can manage their availability.

Instant insurance and agreement validation

Instant feedback on whether the patient’s insurance is compatible with the healthcare professional’s agreements.

04

Design

Low-fidelity wireframes

With the research and personas as a foundation, the next step was to translate complex medical requirements into low-fidelity wireframes.

As soon as the first draft was ready, we held a dedicated feedback session with the founders (the two doctors). This collaborative meeting was essential to make sure the layouts met their clinical standards and real needs.

Design system

This design system was not a mere aesthetic exercise but a strategic tool: a consistent color palette, clear typographic hierarchy rules and a structured set of reusable components.

05

Final layouts

Patient management

A central place to manage personal data, insurance and appointment history. The key differentiator is family member management, which brings the healthcare of children or elderly parents together in a single profile, making care across generations easier.

Fast booking flow

A booking flow optimized to confirm appointments in seconds. Patients can take the fastest path from the search results or get more context on the doctor’s profile, in a flexible and fast process.

Instant insurance and agreement validation

A validation system that automatically checks the patient’s insurance against the doctor’s agreements. Instant visual feedback removes uncertainty about costs and ensures full transparency before the booking is confirmed.

Doctor schedule management

A tool that gives doctors full control of their schedule: update hours, manage appointments across several clinics, share prescriptions with patients and receive real-time notifications of new bookings or cancellations, greatly reducing administrative work.

Administrator management

The platform’s control center for managing users and verifying applications from healthcare professionals. It also tracks appointment status and manages billing, ensuring the quality and operational viability of the ecosystem.

06

Learnings

Bringing healthcare closer to people

Working on a healthcare project side by side with medical professionals was a very rewarding experience. Their constant feedback was essential to build a human, practical tool. For me, this project was personal.

The power of documentation

One of the biggest challenges was defining and documenting the many use cases of three distinct platforms (Patient, Doctor and Administrator).

Growing for future projects

An important lesson: define features in even more detail in the early stages. Even when working in an agile way, I realized that a more detailed technical scope removes ambiguity in web development and prevents scope creep or surprises during the build.

Next steps (post-MVP)

Because of the project timeline and the focus on a lean MVP (Minimum Viable Product), some high-value features were left for future iterations:

  • Teleconsultation: built-in video consultations, to enable remote follow-up of patients with reduced mobility or who live far away.
  • Integrated payments and billing: integrate the SAGE billing platform so administrators can see the subscription status of healthcare professionals.
  • Instant invoicing: automatic invoice issued as soon as the appointment is confirmed or completed.
